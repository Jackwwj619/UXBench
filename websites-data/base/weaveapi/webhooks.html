Webhooks

WeaveAPI sends HTTP POST callbacks to your endpoint when interesting events happen. Each event is signed; verify before processing.

Event types

charge.succeededA charge transitioned to succeeded
charge.failedA charge transitioned to failed
charge.refundedFull or partial refund applied
charge.disputedBuyer opened a dispute
charge.dispute.closedDispute resolved (won or lost)
customer.createdNew customer record

customer.deletedCustomer was deleted
payment_method.attachedPM linked to a customer
payment_method.detachedPM unlinked
invoice.createdNew invoice generated
invoice.finalizedInvoice is no longer draft
invoice.paidInvoice transitioned to paid
invoice.payment_failedCharge attempt failed for invoice
subscription.createdNew subscription
subscription.updatedPlan / quantity / proration change
subscription.canceledSubscription ended

Verifying signatures

Each webhook includes a WeaveAPI-Signature header: t=<unix-ts>,v1=<HMAC-SHA256>. Recompute the HMAC over "<t>.<body>" using your webhook signing secret and constant-time compare.

Retries

We retry non-2xx responses with exponential backoff for up to 72 hours (max 24 attempts). Respond with 200 as soon as the event is enqueued — don't block on heavy work in the request handler.
