    Frequently asked

    Do unused build minutes roll over?

    Free-tier minutes reset monthly and don't roll over. Team and Enterprise have unlimited minutes — no rollover applies.

    What happens if I exceed Free-tier minutes?

    Builds queue. We send an email at 80% and 100% of usage. You can upgrade to Team or buy a one-time top-up at $0.008/min Linux.

    Are there hard caps on Team/Enterprise?

    Team has a soft cap of 8 million minutes/mo before we ask to talk. Enterprise is contractually unlimited.

    Do you offer committed-use discounts?

    Yes — annual pre-purchase of macOS or GPU minutes gets up to 25% off. Enterprise discounts negotiated per workload.

    Where do builds run?

    us-east, us-west, eu-central, eu-west, and ap-southeast by default. EU/UK data residency is available on Team and above.

    Can I run my own runners?

    Yes — self-hosted runners are free on every plan. We charge nothing for orchestration of your hardware.

    How is "concurrent builds" measured?

    Slots running at the same wall-clock instant. Queued jobs don't count.

    Do you integrate with HashiCorp Vault?

    Yes — Vault, Doppler, Infisical, and AWS/GCP/Azure secret managers via OIDC.

    What counts as a "user" on Team?

    Anyone who triggers a build (push, manual run, API). Bot accounts and read-only viewers are free.

    Refunds?

    Pro-rated refund within 14 days of upgrade if you're not happy. Email support@codekite.dev.
