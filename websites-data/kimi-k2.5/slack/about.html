About Slack

Slack is where work happens. We're on a mission to make people's working lives simpler, more pleasant and more productive.

Our Mission

We believe that work should be simpler, more pleasant and more productive. Slack brings all your communication together in one place — it's real-time messaging, archiving and search for modern teams.

Our Story

Founded in 2013, Slack has grown from a small team's internal tool to the platform used by millions of people every day. In 2021, Slack joined Salesforce to bring even more powerful collaboration to teams worldwide.
