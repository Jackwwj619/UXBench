About

Chef Lina Tassi opened Bella Suora in 2019 after twelve years in Roman trattorias. The room is loud, warm, and unapologetically pasta-first; the wood oven runs all service for the half-dozen dishes that earn it. Bar seats are first-come; tables are reservation-only. We grow our own herbs on the back lot — yes, the rosemary on the focaccia.
