Freeze account

A freeze is the strongest, reversible version of "stop everything." We use it for incidents. It is not the same as deleting your account.

What freezing does

  • Every connected app is signed out and rejected until you unfreeze. New tokens cannot be issued.
  • Sign-ins are blocked from every device. You'll need your password and a passkey or backup code to unfreeze.
  • Subscriptions, automatic backups, and recurring data exports are paused.
  • Your data is not deleted. Nothing is shared with apps. You stay billed only if a free trial is converting.

What freezing does not do

  • It does not remove sessions on third-party servers that don't honor token revocation (extremely rare; we report these).
  • It does not freeze accounts you opened with email/password and never linked to MeadowID.

Freeze switch

Once you flip this, every device will be signed out within 60 seconds. Make sure you can unfreeze: have your password and a passkey (or printed backup codes) on hand. If you only have SMS as a second factor, set up something stronger first.

Not frozen

Need something more permanent?

If you're leaving the platform for good, request full deletion. We'll require email confirmation and a 7-day cool-off window before we wipe.
