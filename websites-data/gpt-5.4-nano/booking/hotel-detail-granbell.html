About Shinjuku Granbell Hotel

Located in the heart of Shinjuku, the Granbell Hotel offers modern accommodations just a 5-minute walk from Shinjuku-Sanchome Station. The hotel features stylish rooms with contemporary Japanese design, a rooftop bar with panoramic city views, and easy access to Tokyo's vibrant nightlife and shopping districts.

Each room is thoughtfully designed with comfortable bedding, free WiFi, and modern amenities. The hotel is surrounded by restaurants, convenience stores, and is within walking distance of Kabukicho entertainment district and Shinjuku Gyoen National Garden.

Guests can enjoy the on-site restaurant serving Japanese and international cuisine, relax at the rooftop terrace, and explore the many attractions nearby including Meiji Shrine and Takeshita Street in Harajuku.
