Trust & Security

Security isn't just a feature — it's the foundation of everything we build.

Data Encryption

All data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2+ and at rest using AES-256. Your conversations and files are protected at every layer.

Compliance Certifications

Slack maintains SOC 2 Type II, SOC 3, ISO 27001, ISO 27017, ISO 27018, and CSA certifications. We also support HIPAA and FedRAMP compliance.

Enterprise Key Management

Control your own encryption keys with Slack EKM, powered by AWS KMS. Revoke access to data at any time with full audit trails.

Data Loss Prevention

Native DLP scans messages and files in Slack Connect to prevent sharing of confidential, malicious or personally identifiable content.

Identity & Access

SAML-based SSO, SCIM provisioning, two-factor authentication, session management and domain claiming keep your workspace secure.

Privacy Principles

We don't sell your data. We don't use customer data to train AI models. Your data belongs to you, and we provide tools to manage it.
