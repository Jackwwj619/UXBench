Frequently asked questions

How do I cancel my booking?
You can cancel most bookings directly from your confirmation email or by going to "My trips" and selecting the booking you want to cancel. Cancellation fees depend on the property's policy and how close to check-in you cancel.
When will I receive my refund?
Refunds are typically processed within 5-7 business days after cancellation. The time it takes to appear in your account depends on your bank or payment provider.
Can I change the dates of my booking?
Date changes depend on the property's availability and policy. Go to "My trips", select your booking, and look for the "Change dates" option. If it's not available, you may need to cancel and rebook.
What is Genius and how do I join?
Genius is our free loyalty program. You automatically become a Genius Level 1 member after completing 2 stays within 2 years. Benefits include 10-20% discounts, free breakfast, and room upgrades at participating properties.
I can't find my confirmation email
Check your spam/junk folder first. You can also sign in to your account and view all bookings under "My trips". If you booked without an account, use the booking reference number and email to access your reservation.
How do I contact the property directly?
After booking, you'll find the property's contact details in your confirmation email and in the booking details under "My trips". You can also message the property through our platform.
