Customers

Customers tie payment methods, charges, subscriptions, and invoices to a single record. Use them whenever a buyer might return.

The Customer object

AttributeTypeDescription
idstringcus_…
emailstringRequired for receipts
namestringDisplay name
default_payment_methodstringPM used for off-session charges
balanceintegerCredit balance, applied to next invoice (negative = credit)
currencystringDefault currency
addressobjectBilling address
shippingobjectShipping address (optional)
tax_idsarrayVAT/EIN/etc
metadataobjectUp to 50 keys
createdintegerUnix timestamp

Create a customer

POST /v1/customers

Retrieve a customer

GET /v1/customers/{id}

Update a customer

POST /v1/customers/{id}

Delete a customer

DELETE /v1/customers/{id}

Deletion is permanent. Attached payment methods are detached; historical charges remain.

List customers

GET /v1/customers
