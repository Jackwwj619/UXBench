Frequently asked questions

What is ChatGPT Plus?

ChatGPT Plus is a subscription plan that gives you access to additional features including GPT-4o, o1, DALL-E, Advanced Voice Mode, and higher usage limits.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time. You'll continue to have access to Plus features until the end of your billing period.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, and select digital payment methods depending on your region.

Is there a free trial?

We don't currently offer a free trial for Plus, but you can use ChatGPT for free with limited access to explore the platform before upgrading.
