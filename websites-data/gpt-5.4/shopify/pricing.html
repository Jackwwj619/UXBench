Frequently asked questions

Shopify is a complete commerce platform that lets you start, grow, and manage a business. You can use Shopify to build an online store, manage sales, market to customers, and accept payments in digital and physical locations.

Shopify plans start at £25/month for Basic. You can try any plan free for 3 days, then enjoy £1/month for your first 3 months. No credit card required to start.

Shopify plans are month-to-month by default. You can also choose annual billing for a discount. There are no long-term contracts — you can cancel at any time.

Yes, you can cancel your Shopify account at any time. If you cancel during your free trial, you won't be charged. If you cancel after selecting a plan, your store will remain active until the end of your current billing period.

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from your Shopify admin. Changes take effect immediately, and any price differences are prorated.

If you use Shopify Payments, there are no additional transaction fees. If you use a third-party payment provider, additional fees of 2%, 1%, or 0.6% apply depending on your plan (Basic, Grow, or Advanced).
