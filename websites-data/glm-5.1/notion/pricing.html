Questions & answers

Notion follows standard data protection practices to keep your data encrypted and private. Notion AI will not use your data to train models unless you opt in. AI subprocessors are contractually prohibited from using customer data for training.

Notion currently accepts all major credit and debit card brands.

A block is any single piece of content you add to your page, like a paragraph of text, a to-do item, an image, a code block, an embedded file, etc. Think of your page as being made up of these building blocks.

If you upgrade your workspace to one of the paid plans, you will be charged a fee per member. Guests are free of charge but can only access individual pages they are invited to.

The Plus Plan (with a 1-member limit) is free for students and educators. Just sign up with your school email address and you'll immediately gain access to these features.

If you believe you've made a mistake signing up for a paid subscription, Notion will refund the full amount if you downgrade within three days of signing up for monthly billing, or within 30 days for annual billing.

Your subscription will automatically renew until you cancel it. You can cancel by going to Settings > Billing > Change plan. After canceling, you'll still have access to paid features until the end of your billing cycle.
