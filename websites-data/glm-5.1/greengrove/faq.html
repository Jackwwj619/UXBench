Frequently asked questions

What's the waiting period?

Accidents: 0 days. Illness: 14 days after enrollment. Orthopedic conditions on Oak: 30 days. No waiting on routine wellness for Oak after enrollment.

What counts as a pre-existing condition?

Anything diagnosed or showing clinical signs before your effective date. Curable conditions (e.g., ear infections) are covered again after 180 symptom-free days.

Is there an age limit?

We enroll pets up to 14 years old for cats and small dogs, 10 for large breeds. There's no upper age limit on continuation once enrolled.

Multi-pet discount?

Yes — 10% off each additional pet on the same policy.

Dental?

Dental illness is included on Oak. Periodontal disease is excluded if your pet hadn't had a cleaning in the prior 12 months.

Prescription food?

Covered up to $600/yr on Oak. Not covered on Sprout or Sapling.

Do you require my pet's records before enrollment?

No — we ask at the first claim. We may request the prior 12 months of vet history then.

Can I use any vet?

Yes. Any licensed vet in the US. Specialists, emergency clinics, telehealth — all eligible.

How are claims paid?

Direct deposit, 3–5 business days. Direct-to-vet pay is available for emergencies on Oak.

What's the cancellation policy?

Cancel anytime, pro-rated refund within the first 30 days. After that, refund through the end of the current month.

Bilingual support?

English and Spanish, 24/7. Other languages via translation line during business hours.

Will my rate go up at renewal?

Possibly — rates can adjust for age and local vet-cost inflation. We send a 60-day notice with the new amount.
