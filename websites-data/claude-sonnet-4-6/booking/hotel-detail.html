About Park Hyatt Tokyo

Occupying the top 14 floors of the 52-story Shinjuku Park Tower, Park Hyatt Tokyo offers panoramic views of the city skyline and Mount Fuji. The hotel features the renowned New York Grill restaurant, a 20-meter indoor swimming pool, and the Club on the Park fitness center with a stunning view of the city.

Each room is spacious and elegantly designed with floor-to-ceiling windows, deep soaking tubs, and original artwork. The hotel is located in the Nishi-Shinjuku business district, a 12-minute walk from Shinjuku Station.

Guests can enjoy the Girandole restaurant for all-day dining, the Peak Bar for cocktails with a view, and the Kozue restaurant for authentic Japanese cuisine. The spa offers a range of treatments in a serene setting on the 45th floor.
