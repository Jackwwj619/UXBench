    FAQ

    What counts as "data volume" — compressed or raw?

    Raw uncompressed size of the active dataset, measured monthly P95. Compression and tiering don't reduce your bill until average daily volume drops a full tier for a billing cycle.

    Do you offer committed-use discounts?

    Yes. Annual commit gets ~18% off; 3-year commit ~32% off. Both applied to the matrix base; add-ons follow the same discount.

    Can I downgrade mid-cycle?

    You can move down rows (smaller team) any time; column moves (less data) take effect at the next cycle since we measure data P95 monthly.

    Which regions are supported?

    us-east-1, us-west-2, eu-west-2, eu-central-1, ap-southeast-1, ap-northeast-1. Scale and above can request additional regions.

    How is GPU acceleration billed?

    Per GPU-hour ($1.20). The matrix sidebar assumes ~100 GPU-hours/mo; you only pay what you use.

    Do you charge for egress?

    Free up to 5× your storage tier per month. Above that, $0.08/GB; cross-region replication egress is free between Lattice-managed regions.

    Free trial?

    14 days on Developer-tier cells, no credit card. We send a "we still see you in trial" email at day 12.

    Refund policy?

    Pro-rated refund within 14 days of upgrade. After that, the remainder of the cycle is non-refundable.
